🚀 About IVC @ VVCE

The Innovators & Visionaries Club (IVC) at Vidya Vardhaka College of Engineering is a student-led organization dedicated to bridging academic theory and real-world application.

Technical Workshops

Hands-on sessions on GitHub, Full-Stack, IoT, and AI prototyping.

Project Incubation

Supporting 3D Digital Twins, Cybersecurity tools, and Smart Hardware.

Hackathons

Solving complex problems through national-level technical symposia.

Peer Mentorship

Senior leads sharing knowledge on project management and rapid prototyping.
